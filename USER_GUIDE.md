## 🎨 Onboarding & Creating Your Custom Sports Avatar

Your digital identity on the trail starts with our fully custom **Vector Avatar Builder**. This is more than a profile picture; your custom avatar represents your real-time presence on both the interactive maps and the live AR HUD viewports.

### How to Build Your Avatar:
1. **Accessing the Builder**: Go to the **Profile** tab and tap the **"Redesign Avatar"** button.
2. **Body & Proportions**: Select your athletic silhouette (Slim, Athletic, Muscular, or Average).
3. **Organic Skin Tones**: Pick your organic skin tone from the pigment spectrum.
4. **Activewear & Hair Styling**: Custom-tailor your sports uniform color and choose your athletic hairstyle (Short, Long, Curly, or None) alongside customized hair colors.
5. **High-Performance Accessories**: Equip specialized gear tailored to your workout:
   * **Helmet**: Essential for cycling and mountain biking.
   * **Polarized Shades**: Protects your eyes from visual glare.
   * **Headlamp**: For night running or hiking overlays.
   * **Sports Cap / Headband**: Sleek profiles for hot summer training.
6. **Career Stats**: Set your real athletic level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Elite) and highlight your preferred sport disciplines. Click **"Save Athlete Profile"** to publish.

## 🗺️ Discovering Trails & Nearby Athletes

The **Discover** map serves as your central dashboard.

### Core Map Interactivity:
* **Current Location Sensing**: By default, the map requests browser geolocation permissions to locate you and centers around your coordinates.
* **Trail Pinpoints**: Map markers indicate starting trailheads nearby. Clicking any trail reveals its distance, elevation gain, difficulty, and registered photos.
* **Proximity Radius Indicators**: The map visualizes nearby runners and cyclists using their real-time coordinates. Clicking their floating avatars displays their sports level, current stats, and safety tier.
* **Filter Filters**: Use the top filter buttons to narrow down trail lists by discipline (e.g., Mountain Biking only) or search for specific trails directly using the text search bar.

## 📈 Plotting Routes: Manual Drawing, Live GPS, and GPX Imports

Go to the **Draw Route** tab. Here, you have three flexible, powerful ways to create and publish a new trail.

```
                      ┌───────────────────────────────────────┐
                      │          DRAW ROUTE WORKSPACE         │
                      └──────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                         │
        ┌────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────┐
        ▼                                ▼                                ▼
┌──────────────────────┐      ┌──────────────────────┐      ┌──────────────────────┐
│  Click-to-Draw Map   │      │  Live GPS Recorder   │      │  GPX File Import     │
│  - Tap check-points  │      │  - Geolocator sync   │      │  - Parse Garmin/     │
│  - Auto calculations │      │  - Sandbox simulator │      │    Strava XML logs   │
└──────────────────────┘      └──────────────────────┘      └──────────────────────┘
```

### Option A: Manual Plotting (Click-to-Draw)
1. Tap directly on the interactive map surface.
2. Consecutive taps will connect to draw an exact path.
3. The app computes cumulative metrics, difficulty rankings, and updates a live SVG elevation profile in real-time.
4. Tap **"Undo"** to delete your last waypoint, or **"Reset"** to clear the canvas.

### Option B: Live GPS Recording
1. Tap **"Start GPS Route Recording"**.
2. Walk, run, or ride physically. The system tracks your real-world coordinates and plots them.
3. **Testing Simulator**: If you're on a stationary desktop computer or inside a sandbox, TrailMates launches a simulated tracking loop. This replicates real physical movement along your selected route for instant verification.
4. Tap **"Pause"** or **"Resume"** during your workout, and tap **"Stop & Save"** when you are done.

### Option C: Importing standard GPX Tracks
1. If you co-train with a Garmin watch, Suunto, or Strava, you can import standard GPX tracks.
2. Click the **"Import GPX File"** button in the top menu and select your `.gpx` file.
3. The app parses the file, plots the map, and displays your full elevation profile instantly.
4. When done, name your route, select its discipline, choose whether to publish it to the community, and tap **"Save Route"**.

## 📷 Route Checkpoint Photos & Media Sharing

A key community aspect of TrailMates is logging trail conditions.

### How to Bind Media to Trail Coordinates:
1. Tap any trail on the **Discover** map.
2. The bottom card will slide up, revealing the trail details and its elevation timeline.
3. Tap on any specific waypoint index on the elevation chart.
4. Tap the **Camera icon** ("Add Photo").
5. Upload an image from your device.
6. The image is pinned to those exact coordinates on the map. Other athletes can now click that checkpoint to see trail conditions (e.g., fallen trees, ice, muddy bridges) before setting off!

## 💬 The Social Feed, Active Lobby, and Messaging Suite

The **Feed** tab keeps you connected to your active local athletic network.

### Features of the Social Suite:
* **The Active Trail Lobby**: Found at the top of the feed screen, this indexes local athletes currently on nearby routes. Click **"Join"** to co-train, **"Challenge"** to start a head-to-head sprint, or **"Chat"** to coordinate.
* **Rich Post Composer**: Compose thoughts, attach recorded trails, and upload workout snapshots using the **"Upload Snapshot"** file uploader.
* **Engagement Overlays**: Tap any post to open interactive comment columns, tap to like, or use the quick-express emoji bar to react.
* **Direct Private Messenger**: Click any user in the **People** tab and select **"Chat"** to coordinate meetups or discuss runs with active companions in real-time.

## 🛡️ The Challenger System: Dynamic Friend Tiers & Safety Controls

To safeguard user privacy, TrailMates replaces basic friend lists with distinct safety tiers.

```
       [ ALL USERS IN THE PROXIMITY DIRECTORY ]
                        │
                        ├──────────────────────────┐
                        ▼                          ▼
            ┌──────────────────────┐   ┌──────────────────────┐
            │   CHALLENGER TIER    │   │      MATE / FRIEND   │
            ├──────────────────────┤   ├──────────────────────┤
            │  - Public posts only │   │  - Direct Chat       │
            │  - High scores only  │   │  - Live AR HUD sync  │
            │  - Chat blocked      │   │  - Co-train invites  │
            │  - Location masked   │   │  - Private posts     │
            └──────────────────────┘   └──────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Challenger Tier (Competitive, Low-Visibility)
Place users here if you only want to compete against their trail high-scores. 
* They **cannot** send you direct messages.
* They **cannot** see your real-time proximity.
* They **cannot** view posts marked "Friends Only".

### 2. Mate/Friend Tier (Trusted, High-Visibility)
Place co-trainers and close friends here. They can chat with you, join your active tracking sessions, and view your private post updates.

### Safety Configurations & Blocker Portal:
* **Toggle Post Audiences**: Choose between **Public** or **Friends Only** from the audience selector when writing a post.
* **Instant Profile Block**: Tap any user profile in the directory and click **"Block"**. This isolates your account, hides you from their map view, and removes all trace of communication. You can manage blocked users in the **Blocked** tab of the People screen.
* **Profile Masking**: Go to **Profile** and toggle your profile between **Public** and **Private** to control general search visibility.

## 🕶️ Live Workouts: AR Camera HUD, Sonar Radar, and Sprint Boosts

When you start or join an active workout session, the app opens the **Live Session Portal**:

### 1. Camera AR (Augmented Reality HUD)
Tap the **Camera AR** tab. If you authorize camera permissions, the viewport renders your real camera stream.
* Nearby athletes are rendered as 3D-floating panels.
* Their custom-styled vector avatars float above their physical compass heading, displaying real-time distance and relative altitude offsets.

### 2. The 360° Swivel Compass
If your device's compass is restricted or you are playing in a browser sandbox, drag the **Swivel Compass Slider** at the bottom of the AR view. This simulates rotating your device 360 degrees, allowing you to manually search your surrounding horizon for your training partners' avatars.

### 3. Sonar Radar Grid
Tap the **Radar HUD** tab. This loads an immersive, low-contrast tactical sonar grid.
* The grid sweeps in 25m, 50m, 75m, and 100m concentric rings.
* If your training partner moves closer to you, their avatar sweeps and glides closer to the bullseye in real-time.

### 4. Sprint Push! (Boost Modifier)
Feeling athletic or climbing a steep hill? Click **"Sprint Push!"** to activate a boost. This simulates high-intensity sprint tracking, boosting your distance rate, energy calculation (calories), and coordinates tracking.

### 5. Exporting to Feed
When you finish your run, click **"Finish Run"** to compile your session telemetry. Award career points to your profile stats, export the map to your Feed, and download the finished run as a `.gpx` file!

## ❓ Comprehensive FAQ & Technical Support

### Q1: Why can't a Challenger see my posts?
Challengers are restricted to public data. If you compose a post and set its audience to **"Friends Only"**, only users in your **Friend/Mate** tier will see it. This keeps your daily routines private.

### Q2: Do I need to select a country or region?
No. TrailMates is entirely proximity-based. It uses your physical coordinates to show trails and runners near you, completely bypassing rigid country filters.

### Q3: Why is my AR view blank?
The real-time AR HUD tracks companions within a **100-meter range**. If your co-trainer is further than 100m away, they will not appear on the radar or camera viewport. Get closer to sync!

### Q4: Is my precise location shared with everyone?
No. Your location is never shown to the public. It is only shared in real-time with authorized **Friend/Mate** connections, and only when you are actively co-training in a shared session.

### Q5: How do I import a GPX file?
Go to **Draw Route**, click **"Import GPX File"**, select a standard `.gpx` file from your device, and TrailMates will parse the coordinates and plot the map instantly.

### Q6: Can I block someone from messaging me?
Yes. Demoting a user to **Challenger** revokes messaging privileges. For complete isolation, click **"Block User"** to hide each other's accounts entirely.

### Q7: What does "Sprint Push!" do in the Live Workout?
It triggers a temporary workout boost, simulating a sprint by multiplying your pace, distance rate, and calorie burn in real-time.

### Q8: How can I change my friend's tier?
Go to the **People** tab, select a user, and toggle between **Friend** and **Challenger** to update permissions instantly.

### Q9: What if my browser blocks camera permissions?
The **Camera AR** view will show a sleek, ambient dark tactical HUD. All tracking data, compass heading overlays, distances, and sonar radar sweeps remain 100% active.

### Q10: How do I add a photo to a route checkpoint?
On the **Discover** map, select a trail, click any waypoint dot on the elevation profile chart, click the **Camera** button, and upload your photo.

### Q11: Can I delete waypoints while drawing a route?
Yes. Click **"Undo"** to delete the last point, or **"Reset"** to clear the entire map and start over.

### Q12: How are career profile stats calculated?
When you click **"Finish Run"**, your workout distance, duration, and elevation are processed and added directly to your profile's permanent stats.
